AI Laws Tracker

Federal, state, and international AI laws and legislative proposals — enacted and pending. Compiled from primary sources and legal/policy trackers.

Last compiled: July 31, 2026

Law / Bill Jurisdiction Status Key Date Sponsor / Issuer Summary
Great American AI Act of 2026 (GAAIA) Federal — United States Proposed (Discussion Draft) Jun 4, 2026 Rep. Jay Obernolte (R-CA); Rep. Lori Trahan (D-MA) First comprehensive federal AI framework proposal. Transparency mandates for "large frontier developers" ($500M+ revenue); independent audits; federal preemption of some state laws. Still a discussion draft, not formally introduced.
Federal AI Regulation Moratorium (reconciliation bill provision) Federal — United States Proposed (passed committee) May 2026 House Energy & Commerce Committee Would impose a moratorium on state-level AI regulation and enforcement. Passed out of committee as part of a budget reconciliation bill; not yet enacted.
National Policy Framework for Artificial Intelligence Federal — United States Policy Framework (non-binding) Mar 20, 2026 The White House Administration policy framework urging Congress to replace the state-law patchwork with a single uniform federal approach. Not itself legislation.
AI Data Center Moratorium Act of 2026 Federal — United States Proposed 2026 (introduced) Sen. Bernie Sanders; Rep. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez Would impose an immediate federal moratorium on new AI data centers until strong national safeguards are in place.
Federal Rule of Evidence 707 (proposed) Federal — United States Proposed (judicial rulemaking) 2026 (under review) U.S. Judicial Conference, Committee on Rules of Practice & Procedure Would require a reliability hearing for AI-generated evidence offered at trial without a corresponding human expert.
Texas Responsible AI Governance Act (TRAIGA) State — Texas Enacted Effective Jan 1, 2026 Texas Legislature Intent-based approach: prohibits developing/deploying AI with intent to manipulate, unlawfully discriminate, or infringe rights.
SB 53 State — California Enacted Effective Jan 1, 2026 California Legislature Targets developers of frontier AI models specifically. Transparency/safety-disclosure requirements.
AB 2013 State — California Enacted Effective Jan 1, 2026 California Legislature Companion frontier-model disclosure law enacted alongside SB 53.
Colorado AI Act (SB 24-205) State — Colorado Enacted (amended) Effective Jun 30, 2026 (delayed from Feb 1, 2026) Colorado Legislature Requires "reasonable care" from developers/deployers of high-risk AI systems. Original Act repealed and replaced with a narrower automated-decision law arriving in 2027.
Illinois AI Regulation Bill State — Illinois Enacted Signed Jul 6, 2026 Gov. JB Pritzker Described as the "most comprehensive" state AI safety law to date; mandates annual third-party audits of covered AI systems.
EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) International — European Union Enacted (phased rollout) In force Aug 1, 2024; general application Aug 2, 2026 European Parliament & Council of the EU First comprehensive AI regulatory framework globally. Aug 2, 2026 is the major enforcement milestone: GPAI penalty powers, Article 50 transparency obligations, and full national market-surveillance authority all activate.
EU Digital Omnibus on AI (amendment package) International — European Union Provisional Agreement Agreed May 7, 2026 European Parliament, Council of the EU, European Commission First amendment package to the AI Act since its 2024 adoption. Timeline relief and targeted simplification.

Compiled July 31, 2026. As of March 2026, 45 states had introduced 1,561 AI-related bills; 85 new laws had been enacted across 27 states by late July 2026. This tracker is a representative sample, not exhaustive. For continuously updated tracking, see the NCSL AI Legislation Database.
